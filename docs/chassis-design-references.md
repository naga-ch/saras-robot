# Chassis Design References

The Version 1 chassis design is informed by several common mobile-robot layouts:

- Compact 4WD skid-steer chassis.
- Angular indoor rover bodies.
- Modular robot platforms.
- Foam/form-sheet prototype construction.

The reference designs are being used for general inspiration only. The final Version 1 chassis will be designed specifically for the selected motors, wheels, battery, ESP32, motor drivers, and future Raspberry Pi/LiDAR integration.

## Design direction

The planned appearance is:

- Compact angular rover body.
- Sloped front bumper.
- Partially covered wheels.
- Removable upper deck.
- Central low battery compartment.
- Side-mounted motor drivers.
- Front camera mount.
- Future top-mounted LiDAR platform.
- External emergency stop and power switch.

## Design constraints

- Version 1 will use form sheets.
- Load-bearing components will use mechanical fasteners.
- Foam-safe adhesive will be used only for panel joints.
- Electronics must remain accessible.
- Battery must be secured and mounted low.
- Motors must be mounted using brackets.
- Pi and LiDAR mounting holes will be finalized after the components are received.
